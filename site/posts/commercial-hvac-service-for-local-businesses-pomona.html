Common Problems Businesses See in the Region

Santa Ana winds bring extra dust and debris that clog filters and coils faster than in milder climates. Older commercial buildings around Pomona often have aging rooftop units that struggle during peak summer heat. High electric bills are a frequent complaint because systems run longer to maintain indoor temps when outdoor air is desert-adjacent and dry. In places like Diamond Bar, businesses also report uneven cooling from poorly sealed ducts or low refrigerant levels caused by small leaks that worsen over time.

Simple Steps You Can Take Yourself

Start by checking and replacing filters every 30 to 60 days during heavy use seasons. Keep outdoor condensers clear of debris, leaves, and weeds so airflow stays strong. Walk through your space and note any rooms that stay warmer than others; this can point to simple damper adjustments. Businesses can also set programmable thermostats to avoid running full cooling during non-business hours. These steps help, but they do not replace professional inspections for electrical connections, refrigerant levels, or belt tension.

When AC Repair in Pomona Requires a Professional

Call for AC repair in Pomona if you notice strange noises, ice on coils, or water dripping from the unit. Weak airflow despite clean filters usually means a deeper issue like failing motors or collapsed ducts that homeowners or staff should not attempt to fix. Refrigerant handling requires EPA certification, so leave that to licensed technicians. In Pomona's older commercial stock, wiring and control board problems appear often and need proper diagnostic tools. Regular tune-ups catch these before they lead to full breakdowns during the hottest weeks.
