DIY Steps You Can Try First

Start by checking and replacing your air filter if it looks dirty. This simple step restores airflow in many cases and costs little. Next, ensure outdoor condenser coils are clear of debris, leaves, or dust buildup from recent winds. Confirm the thermostat is set to cooling mode and the temperature is lower than room temperature. If the unit still blows warm air after these checks, avoid further tinkering with refrigerant or electrical components.
