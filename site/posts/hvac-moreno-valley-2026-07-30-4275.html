Moreno Valley HVAC Service: Diagnostics and Maintenance

By BOMING Air · August 3, 2026 · Serving Moreno Valley, CA

Service Call in Moreno Valley

BOMING technicians performed a diagnostic visit on an outdoor HVAC air conditioner unit mounted on a concrete pad. The work included visual inspection of the system, checking the air filter condition, and connecting manifold gauges to measure refrigerant pressures with R410A.

Tools Used on Site

Technicians used a Fieldpiece SMAN digital manifold gauge to record system readings. A thermal camera was positioned near an HVAC vent to examine temperature distribution while wearing protective gloves. These steps help identify performance issues without assuming specific repairs or results.

Relevance for Moreno Valley Homeowners

Southern California summers place heavy demands on air conditioning systems. Regular checks of filters, refrigerant levels, and airflow support consistent operation and help manage energy use in hot inland areas like Moreno Valley. Dirty filters and inaccurate pressures can affect system efficiency over time.
