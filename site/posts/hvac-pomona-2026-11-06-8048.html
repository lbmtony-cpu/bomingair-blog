Pomona Wall AC Installation: New Tosot System

By BOMING Air · November 7, 2026 · Serving Pomona, CA

Project Assessment

BOMING technicians began with thermal imaging to evaluate the existing wall AC setup in the Pomona home. This step identified heat patterns and confirmed the need for a complete new system rather than repairs.

Equipment Installation

The team installed a new white Tosot wall-mounted indoor unit on a beige wall, set to display 73 degrees. An outdoor condenser was placed on a concrete pad beside a brick wall, completing the full installation of new equipment in November 2026.

Value for Pomona Homeowners

Pomona summers demand reliable cooling. This ductless installation improves efficiency, reduces energy use, and provides quiet operation suited to Southern California homes. Professional placement ensures proper airflow and longevity.
