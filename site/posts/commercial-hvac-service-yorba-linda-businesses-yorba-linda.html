Simple Maintenance Steps You Can Handle

Replace filters every 30 to 45 days during summer. Keep outdoor condensers clear of debris and at least two feet from walls or plants. Check that supply and return vents stay unobstructed by furniture or displays.

  • Log monthly temperature readings at different times of day to spot trends early.
  • Inspect belts for cracks and tension once per quarter if you have rooftop access.
  • Clear drain lines with a wet-vac to prevent water backup and ceiling damage.

These steps reduce minor service calls but do not replace professional coil cleaning or electrical testing.
