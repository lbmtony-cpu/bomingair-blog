How SCE and SoCalGas Programs Work for HVAC Upgrades

Southern California Edison offers incentives for high-efficiency heat pumps and air conditioners that meet specific SEER ratings. SoCalGas provides rebates on furnaces and hybrid systems that reduce natural gas use. These programs target equipment that lowers overall energy demand during peak heat. Start by checking your current system age and efficiency on the utility websites or by scheduling an assessment. Steps to Apply for Rebates Without Guesswork

First, confirm your zip code qualifies and review the current qualified products list on the SCE or SoCalGas site. Next, get a load calculation from a licensed contractor rather than guessing equipment size, since oversized units waste energy in dusty local conditions. Submit the rebate form after installation with the invoice and model numbers. Keep records of your old system for proof of replacement. If the project involves both electric and gas equipment, you may combine incentives from both utilities.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to replace my whole system to get SCE rebates?

Most rebates require replacing the outdoor condenser and indoor coil together for full efficiency ratings. Partial replacements rarely qualify.

How long does the rebate process take in Ontario?

Approval usually happens within four to six weeks after you submit all paperwork and the contractor verifies installation.

Can I combine SCE and SoCalGas incentives?

Yes, when your project includes both electric and gas equipment that each meet program requirements.

What if my home is older and has poor insulation?

Rebates focus on equipment efficiency, but adding insulation or sealing ducts can improve results and may have separate utility programs.
