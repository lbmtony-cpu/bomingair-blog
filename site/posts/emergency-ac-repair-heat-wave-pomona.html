By BOMING Air · July 31, 2026 · Serving Pomona, CA

Why AC Failures Spike in Pomona Heat Waves

When temperatures spike, many residents suddenly need AC repair in Pomona to keep homes livable. Pomona sits in a desert-adjacent part of Southern California where hot, dry summers combine with dust and occasional Santa Ana winds that push fine particles into outdoor units. Older housing stock common in the area often has aging equipment that struggles under continuous load, driving up electric bills fast.

Common causes include clogged filters that restrict airflow, low refrigerant from small leaks, dirty condenser coils coated in dust, and capacitors that fail when the system runs nonstop. These issues worsen during multi-day heat waves when the unit never gets a break.

Immediate Steps to Stay Cool

Close blinds and curtains on south- and west-facing windows to block solar gain. Run ceiling fans to move air and set your thermostat a few degrees higher than usual if the AC is struggling. Check and replace the indoor air filter if it looks dirty—this simple step often restores some cooling capacity quickly.

Move portable fans near open interior doors to help circulate cooler air from any working vents. Avoid using ovens or dryers during peak heat, as they add extra load to the system. These steps buy time but do not fix underlying problems.

DIY Checks You Can Safely Perform

Turn off power at the breaker before inspecting the outdoor unit. Clear leaves, grass clippings, and visible debris from around the condenser, keeping at least two feet of clearance. Rinse the coils gently with a garden hose on a low setting if they are caked with dust—never use high pressure or cleaners unless you have experience.

Listen for unusual noises like grinding or clicking when the system tries to start. Note whether the indoor blower runs but no cool air comes out, or if nothing runs at all. These observations help a technician diagnose faster, but stop and call a pro if you smell burning or see sparks.

When Professional AC Repair in Pomona Is Required

Call for AC repair in Pomona right away if the system blows warm air, cycles on and off rapidly, or shows error codes on a digital thermostat. Refrigerant issues, electrical failures, and compressor problems require licensed tools and EPA certification that homeowners do not have. Attempting these repairs yourself can void warranties or create safety hazards.

Technicians in the area are familiar with the specific challenges of Pomona homes and the nearby cities of Ontario, Claremont, and Upland, where similar heat and dust conditions apply. They can recharge refrigerant, replace failing parts, and check for duct leaks common in older construction.

Preventing Repeat Failures in SoCal Summers

Schedule annual maintenance before summer arrives to catch small issues early. Keep vegetation trimmed away from the outdoor unit year-round and consider adding a shade structure that still allows airflow. Upgrade to a higher-efficiency filter if your system can handle it, and have ducts inspected if your home is more than twenty years old.

Monitor your electric bill for sudden spikes, which often signal an inefficient system. Simple habits like programming the thermostat to rise when no one is home reduce strain during the hottest parts of the day. These steps extend equipment life in Pomona's demanding climate.
