Why Indoor Air Quality Matters in Norco's Climate

Living in Norco means dealing with hot, dry summers, occasional Santa Ana winds that stir up dust, and older housing stock that can trap allergens. These conditions make indoor air quality a real concern for families and business owners. When your AC system struggles, pollen, dust mites, and pet dander circulate more easily, leading to stuffy noses and irritated eyes. Scheduling AC repair in Norco early can restore proper airflow and filtration before problems worsen.

Clogged filters fail to catch mold spores that thrive in humid AC coils. Older homes often have carpet or upholstery that holds allergens for months. DIY Steps to Reduce Allergens

Start with what you can do yourself. Replace your HVAC filter every 30 to 60 days during peak summer use, choosing a MERV 8 or higher rated filter that fits your system. Vacuum with a HEPA filter weekly and wash bedding in hot water. Keep indoor humidity between 40 and 50 percent with a simple hygrometer. Close blinds during the hottest part of the day to reduce the load on your AC. These small habits cut allergen levels noticeably without calling anyone.

  • Check and clean return air vents monthly
  • Use allergen-proof pillow and mattress covers
  • Run kitchen and bath exhaust fans while cooking or showering
  • Store firewood and outdoor shoes away from living areas

How often should I change my filter in Norco?

Is a higher MERV filter always better?
