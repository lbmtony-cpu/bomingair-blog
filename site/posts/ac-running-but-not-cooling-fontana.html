AC Repair in Fontana: Why Your AC Runs But Won't Cool

By BOMING Air · October 14, 2026 · Serving Fontana, CA

If your air conditioner is blowing air but the house stays hot in Fontana, you need reliable AC repair in Fontana to pinpoint the problem fast. The region's hot dry summers, dust from nearby deserts, and occasional Santa Ana winds can quickly reduce cooling performance in homes and businesses across Fontana, Ontario, Rancho Cucamonga, and Pomona.

Dirty or Clogged Air Filter

A restricted filter is the most common reason an AC runs without cooling. Dust and pollen build up fast during dry SoCal summers, blocking airflow over the evaporator coil. The system still runs but moves little cool air. Check the filter monthly and replace every 30-90 days. Homeowners can handle this easily with standard 1-inch or 2-inch filters available at local stores.

Thermostat Placement or Settings

Thermostats mounted in direct sunlight or near heat sources may read incorrectly. In older Fontana housing stock, outdated thermostats can also fail to signal the system properly. Try setting the fan to auto and lowering the temperature 3-5 degrees to test. If the unit still runs constantly without cooling, the thermostat may need calibration or replacement by a technician during AC repair in Fontana.

Low Refrigerant or Leaks

Refrigerant does not get used up; low levels point to a leak in the coils or lines. The compressor keeps running but cannot absorb enough heat, leaving indoor air warm. This requires professional leak detection and repair. DIY refrigerant handling is illegal and dangerous, so schedule AC repair in Fontana as soon as you notice ice on the indoor lines or hissing sounds.

Dirty Outdoor Condenser Coils

The condenser sits outside and collects dust, leaves, and pet hair. When fins are coated, heat cannot escape efficiently. Clean the coils gently with a garden hose on low pressure once a year before peak summer heat arrives. Severe buildup or bent fins in areas like Ontario and Rancho Cucamonga usually need a pro with proper coil cleaner and fin combs.

Capacitor or Electrical Problems

Start capacitors and run capacitors help the compressor and fan motors turn on. Heat and age cause them to weaken, so the AC runs but the compressor struggles to cool. A clicking sound or warm air after 10-15 minutes often indicates this issue. These parts are inexpensive but require safe electrical work, making them a job for licensed AC repair in Fontana technicians.

When to Skip DIY and Call a Pro

Attempt basic filter changes and thermostat checks yourself. Stop and contact experts if you see ice, hear unusual noises, notice high humidity indoors, or face electric bills that have jumped 30 percent or more. Older homes in Pomona and surrounding areas often have outdated ductwork that leaks cooled air into attics. A full diagnostic during AC repair in Fontana can identify these hidden problems and restore efficient cooling without guesswork.
