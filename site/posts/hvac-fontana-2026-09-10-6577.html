Fontana AC Repair: Control Board and Ductwork Service

By BOMING Air · September 11, 2026 · Serving Fontana, CA

Initial System Assessment

A Fontana homeowner requested AC and heating repair on existing equipment. Our technicians inspected the outdoor unit control board, exposed wiring, and interior circuit boards showing alarm codes. The alarm guide manual helped identify relay and connection faults without replacing the full system.

Ductwork and Insulation Review

Attic inspection revealed HVAC ductwork with insulation gaps common in Southern California homes. Proper sealing prevents conditioned air loss during extreme summer heat and winter temperature swings, improving efficiency for the existing setup.

Control Board and Wiring Repair

Technicians addressed faulty relays and wiring connections on the control board. This targeted repair restored communication between components while preserving the original equipment, avoiding unnecessary full-unit replacement.
