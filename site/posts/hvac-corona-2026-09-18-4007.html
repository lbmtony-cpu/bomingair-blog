Corona Installation: New Equipment with SIL-FOS 15

By BOMING Air · September 19, 2026 · Serving Corona, CA

The Corona Installation Project

In September 2026, a new HVAC system was installed in a Corona residence. The project focused on proper setup of new equipment, including refrigerant line connections completed with SIL-FOS 15 brazing rods. This ensured secure, leak-resistant joints from the start.

Materials and Process Details

SIL-FOS 15 rods were selected for their 15% silver content, providing strong bonds on copper tubing during the installation. Photos from the job site show these rods on retail shelves next to drill bits, confirming the materials used for precise fitting and drilling tasks. All work followed manufacturer guidelines for new equipment placement and line routing.

Corona summers demand reliable cooling.
