Eastvale AC Repair: Thermal Imaging and Gauge Service

By BOMING Air · September 18, 2026 · Serving Eastvale, CA

Thermal Imaging Assessment of Airflow

Technicians began the Eastvale service call by using a thermal camera to inspect ceiling air vents on the existing HVAC system. This non-invasive step revealed temperature variations that pointed to restricted airflow or insulation gaps common in Southern California homes.

Outdoor Diagnostics with Precision Tools

Outside, the team set up manifold gauges, hoses, and a refrigerant scale in a blue case beside the unit. Readings from the gauges helped identify pressure imbalances without replacing any equipment, focusing solely on restoring proper function to the current system.
