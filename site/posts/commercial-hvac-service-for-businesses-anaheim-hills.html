Common Issues Faced by Businesses

Dust from dry winds is a top culprit in Anaheim Hills commercial spaces. It clogs filters and coils faster than in milder coastal zones, reducing airflow and forcing compressors to work overtime. High summer electric bills often trace back to this buildup plus low refrigerant levels or worn belts that go unnoticed until a unit stops cooling entirely.

Another frequent problem is uneven temperatures across large retail or office areas. Poorly balanced dampers or leaking ducts allow hot attic air to mix in, especially when Santa Ana winds create pressure differences. In buildings with older stock, corroded condensate drains can also cause water damage to ceilings or inventory if left unchecked.

Maintenance Tips for Local Commercial Properties

Start with monthly filter checks during summer months. Swap them when they look gray rather than waiting for the recommended schedule, since dust loads are heavier here. Keep outdoor condenser units clear of debris and at least two feet from walls or landscaping to maintain proper airflow.

  • Inspect belts and pulleys quarterly for cracks or slippage.
  • Schedule coil cleaning before May to handle the coming heat wave season.
  • Monitor thermostat settings to avoid overcooling empty zones overnight.

These steps help cut energy use noticeably. However, electrical diagnostics, refrigerant handling, and duct sealing require EPA-certified pros and specialized tools.
