AC Repair in Ontario: Leaky Ducts Waste Cooling & Raise Bills

By BOMING Air · September 15, 2026 · Serving Ontario, CA

The Impact of Leaky or Dirty Ducts on Your Cooling Bills

Southern California summers bring intense heat that pushes air conditioners hard. When ducts leak or fill with dust, cooled air escapes into attics or walls instead of reaching your rooms. This forces your system to run longer, driving up electric bills. Homeowners in Ontario often notice this during peak heat when Santa Ana winds stir extra dust into the system.

Leaky ducts can lose 20-30% of conditioned air in typical homes. Dirty ducts restrict airflow, making the AC work harder and wear out faster. In dry climates like ours, this combination quickly adds hundreds to summer utility costs.

Why This Is Common in Ontario and Nearby Areas

Many homes in Ontario, Pomona, and Rancho Cucamonga were built decades ago with duct systems that have aged. Older housing stock often has unsealed joints, thin insulation, and flexible ducts that sag or disconnect over time. High summer electric bills are common here because desert-adjacent heat combines with dust from winds to clog filters and coils.

Business owners in these areas face the same issues in commercial spaces where ducts run through unconditioned areas. Regular AC repair in Ontario calls frequently trace back to duct problems rather than the equipment itself.

Signs Your Ductwork Needs Attention

Uneven temperatures between rooms are a clear clue. If some areas stay warm while others cool quickly, air is likely escaping. High dust levels on vents or musty odors point to dirt buildup inside the ducts.

Listen for whistling or rattling when the system runs. Rising bills without changes in usage also suggest inefficiency. In Ontario homes, these signs appear more often after windy seasons that push fine particles through the system.

What Homeowners Can Check Themselves

Start with visual inspection of accessible ducts in the attic or crawlspace. Look for gaps at joints or disconnected sections. Replace your air filter monthly during heavy use to reduce dirt entering the ducts.

Feel for air leaks around registers by holding a tissue nearby while the AC runs. Seal obvious gaps with foil tape or mastic if you are comfortable working in those spaces. Avoid attempting full cleaning yourself, as improper methods can damage the system or spread contaminants.

Professional Solutions for Duct Problems

Technicians performing AC repair in Ontario use specialized tools to test duct leakage and measure airflow. They seal leaks with approved materials and clean interiors using HEPA vacuums when needed. Adding insulation to ducts in hot attics prevents further energy loss in our climate.

For homes in Pomona or Upland with older systems, pros may recommend duct modifications or zoning to improve distribution. These steps restore efficiency without replacing the entire AC unit.

Improving Efficiency in Southern California's Climate

Addressing ducts helps your cooling system handle hot dry summers and dust from Santa Ana winds more effectively. Proper sealing and cleaning reduce runtime, lower bills, and extend equipment life. Regular maintenance prevents small issues from becoming major repairs.

If you notice persistent problems, schedule a thorough inspection rather than guessing at fixes.
