Why Regular Maintenance Extends AC Lifespan

Simple DIY Steps Homeowners Can Handle

Start with monthly filter changes during heavy use periods. Use the correct MERV rating for your system and replace pleated filters every 30 days when temperatures stay above 90 degrees. Clean around the outdoor condenser by removing leaves and trimming plants at least two feet away to maintain airflow.

Check the thermostat batteries twice a year and ensure supply vents remain unobstructed by furniture. These tasks prevent common problems without special tools. However, avoid opening panels or handling refrigerants yourself, as improper handling can void warranties and create safety risks.

Common Issues Triggered by SoCal Conditions

Santa Ana winds carry extra dust that settles inside cabinets and on fan blades, unbalancing them over time. Another frequent problem is restricted drainage from mineral buildup in the condensate line, leading to water damage if ignored. Addressing these through scheduled service prevents cascading failures that turn a repair into a full replacement.
