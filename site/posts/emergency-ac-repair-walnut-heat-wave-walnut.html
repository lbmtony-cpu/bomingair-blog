Common Causes of Sudden Breakdowns

Most emergency calls during heat waves trace back to a few repeatable issues. Clogged air filters reduce airflow and cause the evaporator coil to freeze, shutting the system down. Dirty condenser coils on the outside unit make the compressor work harder until it overheats and trips the safety switch. Low refrigerant from small leaks that went unnoticed all spring suddenly becomes critical when the unit cannot maintain temperature. Electrical problems such as failing capacitors or loose connections also surface when the system runs at full load for extended periods.

Walnut’s mix of older housing stock and newer developments means both 20-year-old units and recently installed systems can fail under the same stress.

Quick Checks Homeowners Can Handle Safely

Before calling for help, a few safe steps can sometimes restore cooling quickly. Turn the thermostat to off and the fan to on for 30 minutes to let any ice melt. Check and replace the indoor filter if it looks gray or clogged. Clear leaves, dirt, and grass clippings from around the outdoor unit so air can flow freely. Make sure the disconnect switch near the unit is fully on and that breakers in the panel have not tripped.

These steps are fine for basic maintenance. Anything involving refrigerant, wiring inside the unit, or diagnosing error codes should be left to a licensed technician.

When to Call a Professional Right Away

If the system still will not cool after the simple checks, or if you notice warm air from vents, strange noises, or burning smells, stop running the unit and contact a pro. Continuing to run a failing compressor can cause expensive damage. In Walnut, same-day emergency AC repair in Walnut is often needed when a heat wave coincides with multiple system failures across the area. Preventing Repeat Problems in Southern California Homes

After the emergency passes, simple habits reduce the chance of another failure. Change filters every 30 to 60 days during peak summer. Schedule annual tune-ups before June so coils are clean and refrigerant levels are correct. Consider adding a whole-house attic fan or improving attic insulation to lower the load on the AC. In areas with heavy dust, upgrading to a higher-MERV filter or adding a filter grille can help protect the system.

These steps do not eliminate the need for professional service, but they keep equipment running longer in our demanding climate.
