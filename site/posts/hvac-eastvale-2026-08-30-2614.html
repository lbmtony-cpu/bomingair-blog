By BOMING Air · September 4, 2026 · Serving Eastvale, CA

Initial Diagnosis in Eastvale Home

Technicians arrived to address performance issues on an existing AC and heating system. A thermal camera was used to inspect wall HVAC vents, revealing temperature inconsistencies that pointed to restricted airflow or underlying component stress common in Eastvale's hot summers.

Electrical Repair on Outdoor Unit

The outdoor unit showed clear signs of burnt wiring. Gauges manifold tools were connected to measure system pressures while the damaged wiring was repaired. This step restored safe electrical connections without replacing the entire unit, preventing potential shutdowns during peak cooling demand.

Refrigerant System Service

HVAC refrigerant scale and charging hoses were deployed to verify and adjust refrigerant levels. Proper charging ensures the system operates efficiently, reducing strain on the compressor and supporting consistent heating and cooling cycles throughout the year.
