Existing Furnace Condition in Chino Closet

A licensed technician from BOMING Air Conditioning & Heating arrived to service an existing furnace located in a closet with its vent pipe intact. Repairing Damaged Foam Insulation

The visible issue involved damaged foam insulation on the pipes. The technician carefully examined the compromised sections held during inspection.
